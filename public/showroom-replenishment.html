Reconcile from Floor List

Upload an .xlsx or .csv file listing the SKUs currently hanging in the front showroom. The app will set those SKUs to 1 unit at front, rest at back in Shopify, and put all other SKUs at the back. No physical movement required — this just makes Shopify match what's already on your floor.

⚠️ This will issue inventory adjustments to your live Shopify store. Always run the preview first and double-check the totals before clicking Apply.
