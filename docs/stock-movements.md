# Stock movements

Move Stock now supports current Shopify inventory even when no audited physical-count baseline has been provisioned. It resolves each unique tracked SKU to its Shopify inventory item and the existing Display/Warehouse location mapping. Both locations must be distinct and stocked for that item.

Display is one whole location: new requests have no Display rack, and a Display-to-Display rack transfer is rejected. Warehouse choices and dashboard/stock-search references come from the owner-approved `RIS SHEET NEW UPDATE.xlsx`, Warehouse row 1. Multiple racks for the same SKU are preserved. Case and whitespace are normalized for exact SKU matching. The imported reference includes the original file SHA-256 and its effective timestamp.

In live mode, available quantity is checked by Shopify location. Staff select an actual warehouse rack, physically verify the pieces and submit for approval. The sheet updates rack references only; it never overwrites Shopify available/on-hand quantities or creates a reconciled movement baseline. After approval, movement history adjusts the rack references from the immutable sheet. Pending/cancelled/uncertain movements and earlier history do not alter them; repeated reads/retries cannot apply an approved movement twice. A source discrepancy is flagged for rack verification rather than inventing stock.

Legacy reviewed Display positions are summed into one position when loaded, preserving total pieces. Existing movement/care history keeps the original recorded racks, and old Display cleaning/damage/disposal reservations remain included across all previous racks. Reviewed Warehouse positions retain their existing audited count; the new reference does not silently replace that baseline's quantities. The older manual/RIS rack store remains intact for its audit, while inventory browsing and stock search use the new source.

Submission creates a pending movement and reserves its source quantity in the movement form. A SKU with a pending, uncertain or correction-required movement cannot be submitted again or used for a conflicting cleaning operation. Shopify availability remains unchanged until approval. Owner/admin, existing STOCK_MOVEMENT_APPROVERS, and the named inventory lead `tushar` (while holding Inventory or Warehouse access) can review. These authorised approvers may approve their own or other employees' requests. Other staff and read-only accounts cannot approve. History retains the submitter, reviewer and timestamps, and labels self-approved movements.

An approver can cancel an awaiting-approval live request with a reason, including their own request. Cancellation changes no Shopify quantities and releases the pending reservation, preserving the request, cancellation actor, reason and time in History. A replacement request may be created for that SKU. If the pieces have already physically moved, do not move them twice. Audited-position movements and requests with an attempted Shopify dispatch use the existing correction/recovery process instead of direct cancellation.

Pending approval and History use the local register without waiting for a full Shopify inventory read. Approval still checks the current source and destination quantities before writing. The movement form requires a successful live stock read. Delayed catalogue responses cannot overwrite newer review results, and successful submissions open Pending approval immediately.

Cross-location approval reads current Shopify source/destination availability, checks sufficient stock, persists the exact compare-and-set quantities and idempotency key, then updates both locations atomically. Approval invalidates the shared inventory snapshot and signals dashboard tabs to refresh. The dashboard, care register and stock search therefore read the resulting current quantities. Same-location rack transfers only update the movement history and do not change Shopify stock.

Unknown Shopify responses remain sync-pending; retry uses the same input and key. Never cancel an uncertain dispatched request. Retries stop after 23 hours and require inventory-history reconciliation. A known compare-and-set rejection may be marked for physical correction. After an authorised approver verifies the pieces returned to the original source rack, resolving the correction cancels the unconfirmed move and releases its reservation. This does not write stock to Shopify.

Persistent storage remains stock_movements.json beside DATA_PATH, overridable via STOCK_MOVEMENTS_PATH. Existing approved-baseline stores keep their original counted-position workflow. Do not hand-set baseline.reconciled: true. A future audited per-rack ledger still requires sales/receipt integration and reconciliation; live mode makes no claim that its rack balances have been audited.

Keyboard scanners enter exact SKUs. Scanner Enter focuses the quantity field and never submits. Forms require an exact SKU, positive whole-piece quantity, sufficient source availability, different destination and physical confirmation in live mode. Changes to SKU, quantities, locations or racks reset that confirmation.
